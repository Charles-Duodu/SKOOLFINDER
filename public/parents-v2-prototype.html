CLEAR INFORMATION. CLEAR LIMITS.

Know what is behind every school profile.

A published profile, a school-confirmed detail, and a regulatory licence check are distinct things. Our provenance labels make that clear.

01

School-provided information

Details supplied directly by the institution, with the last-updated date and administrative contact recorded for transparency.

02

Profile details checked

Specific operational attributes independently reviewed by SkoolFinder, including campus existence, contact validity, and curriculum.

03

Regulatory licensing information

Independent check against the National Schools Inspectorate Authority (NaSIA) official register of licensed pre-tertiary institutions.

SkoolFinder verifies profile information but is not a licensing authority. You can verify licensing directly on NaSIA's official portal at nasia.gov.gh.

A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Simple, honest answers about exploring schools and using the information on SkoolFinder Ghana.

Is searching for a school free on SkoolFinder?

Yes, completely free. You can search, filter by curriculum, location, and level, view public school profiles, and compare schools without paying any fee or subscription.

Do I need to create an account to browse?

No. You can freely explore as a guest. Creating a free parent account is optional—it gives you convenience tools to save shortlists, bookmark favourite schools, and save comparison tables across your devices.

Are school fees on profiles exact and official quotes?

School fees vary by academic year, grade level, day vs boarding status, and optional services like transport or meals. SkoolFinder provides published fee guidance where verified, but we always recommend requesting a current, written fee schedule directly from the school.

Does SkoolFinder license or accredit schools?

No. School licensing, inspection, and regulatory accreditation in Ghana is conducted exclusively by the National Schools Inspectorate Authority (NaSIA). SkoolFinder provides directory verification and links directly to official licensing databases.

How does the school comparison tool work?

When browsing schools, click the "+ Compare" button on any school card. A bottom dock will track your selections. Once you have chosen 2 or 3 schools, click "Compare choices" to view a side-by-side matrix of their curriculum, pupil-teacher ratios, and facilities.

Can I find private tutors and sports coaches?

Yes. Our Education Professionals section connects families with qualified tutors, music teachers, swimming coaches, and STEM specialists. Professional discovery is linked directly alongside your school search.
