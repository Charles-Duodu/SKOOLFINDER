SKOOLFINDER GHANA — PARENTS & GUARDIANS SIX-SECTION DESIGN PREVIEW
Version: standalone concept v3 · 9 November 2026

WHAT THIS IS
A standalone, interactive design prototype for the public Parents & Guardians landing page.
The page intentionally combines twelve former areas into six public-facing content sections:
1. Hero & school discovery by need
2. Discover & compare schools
3. Two free access options
4. Admissions & parent guidance
5. Tutors & specialist educators
6. Trust, FAQ & final call to action

WHAT WORKS IN THE PREVIEW
Local demo school search (text, level, curriculum); category tabs and filters;
fictional school cards; guest comparison; sample profile & guide dialogs;
mobile navigation; FAQ accordion; responsive desktop/tablet/mobile layouts.

DATA & CONNECTIONS
All school records are FICTIONAL. Images are illustrative, not photographs
of the fictional school campuses. The preview does not connect to a real API,
database, account service, tutor booking, payment, analytics or live website.
Buttons requiring existing production services open explanatory preview dialogs.

HOW TO VIEW
A) The single standalone HTML file can be opened in a browser and contains
   embedded CSS, JS and image assets. It does not need this folder.
B) For editable source, keep index.html, style.css, app.js and assets/ together.
   Open index.html in a browser, or serve the folder from a local development server.

DEPLOYMENT / CHANGE CONTROL
Nothing has been committed to a Git repository or deployed to Vercel.
The registered Parents Portal and other SkoolFinder services are NOT modified.
Do not deploy fictional school examples as live school listings.
Before implementation, conduct the read-only Phase 0 audit in blueprint v2.0.
