A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Answers before you begin.

Straightforward guidance to make exploring Greater Accra schools effortless.

Is searching for schools free?+

Yes, 100% free. You can search, filter, and compare all accredited pre-tertiary schools across Greater Accra without paying any subscription.

Do I need to register to compare schools?+

No. Side-by-side comparison is available to all guest visitors. Creating an account is completely optional for saving searches and bookmarks.

Are school fees exact official quotes?+

School fees vary by academic year, entry grade, day vs boarding status, and optional services. SkoolFinder provides verified guidance, but parents should always request an itemized fee schedule directly from the school.

Does SkoolFinder license or accredit schools?+

No. School licensing, inspection, and regulatory accreditation in Ghana is conducted exclusively by the National Schools Inspectorate Authority (NaSIA).
